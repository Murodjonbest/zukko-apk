1-QADAM. Dasturchi hisobi (bir marta)
  • play.google.com/console → ro'yxatdan o'ting. Bir martalik to'lov 25 $.
  • Shaxsni tasdiqlash (pasport) talab qilinadi. Bir necha kun olishi mumkin.
  • MUHIM: yangi SHAXSIY hisoblar uchun Google talabi bor. Ilovani hammaga chiqarishdan oldin
    kamida 12 ta sinovchi bilan 14 kun "Closed testing" o'tkazish kerak. Tashkilot (yuridik shaxs)
    hisobida bu talab yo'q. Sinovchilar sifatida tanishlaringizning Gmail manzillarini qo'shasiz.

2-QADAM. Maxfiylik siyosati havolasi
  • privacy-policy.html ichidagi EMAIL@MANZIL.UZ ni o'z emailingizga almashtiring
    (shu faylda va app/src/main/assets/privacy.html da).
  • Faylni ochiq havolaga joylang: Google Sites, GitHub Pages yoki o'z saytingiz.
    Shu havola Play Console'ga kiritiladi.

3-QADAM. Android Studio'da AAB yasash
  • Android Studio'ni o'rnating (bepul): developer.android.com/studio
  • File → Open → shu "Zukko-Play" papkasi. Birinchi ochilishda kerakli qismlarni yuklaydi (10–20 daqiqa).
  • Build → Generate Signed App Bundle / APK → "Android App Bundle" → Next
  • "Create new..." bilan kalit (keystore) yarating:
      - Saqlash joyi: masalan D:\Zukko\zukko-upload.jks
      - Parolni yozib qo'ying!
      - Alias: zukko
  • release → Create. Tayyor fayl: app/release/app-release.aab
  !!! zukko-upload.jks faylini va parolni yo'qotmang: har bir yangilanish shu kalit bilan imzolanadi.
      Fleshka yoki bulutga zaxira nusxa oling.

4-QADAM. Play Console'da ilova yaratish
  • "Create app" → nomi: Zukko → til: O'zbek → App → Free.
  • Store listing: DOKON_MATNLARI.txt dagi matnlar, ikonka, banner va skrinshotlar.
  • App content bo'limi:
      - Privacy policy: 2-qadamdagi havola
      - Ads: "No, my app does not contain ads"
      - App access: "All functionality is available without special access"
      - Content rating: so'rovnomani to'ldiring (zo'ravonlik yo'q, ta'lim). Natija: "3+ / Everyone"
      - Target audience: 13+ yoki "Barcha yoshlar" (bolalar rejimi bor). Agar 13 yoshdan
        kichiklarni tanlasangiz, ilova "Families" siyosatiga mos bo'lishi kerak. Zukko bunga mos:
        reklama yo'q, ma'lumot yig'ilmaydi, internet yo'q.
      - Data safety: "No data collected", "No data shared". Shifrlash savoliga: "Data is not transmitted".
      - Government app: No. Financial features: None. Health: No.
  • Testing → Closed testing → yangi reliz → app-release.aab ni yuklang → sinovchilarni qo'shing.
  • 14 kundan keyin: Production → "Promote release" → Review uchun yuborish (odatda 1–7 kun).

5-QADAM. Yangilash (keyingi versiyalar)
  • app/build.gradle da versionCode ni 1 ga oshiring (2, 3, ...), versionName ni yangilang (1.0.1).
  • 3-qadamdagi kabi AAB yasang, xuddi shu kalit (zukko-upload.jks) bilan imzolang.
